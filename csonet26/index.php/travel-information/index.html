Venue & Travel Information

Venue

CSoNet 2026 will be held at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam National University – Ho Chi Minh City, on November 16–18, 2026.

Address: Ly Thuong Kiet Campus, 268 Ly Thuong Kiet Street, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
Location: View on Google Maps
Website: hcmut.edu.vn

All technical sessions, keynotes, workshops, special sessions, and the panel discussion take place on campus, in the main hall and five (05) session rooms. The Conference Banquet will be held on the evening of November 17, 2026 (18:30–21:30) at Capella Gallery Hall, Ho Chi Minh City.

Getting There

The nearest airport is Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), about 5 km from HCMUT. The ride takes roughly 20–40 minutes, depending on traffic. International flights arrive at Terminal 2.

Ride-hailing apps: Grab, Xanh SM and Be all operate at the airport. Pick-up points are signposted outside the arrivals hall.

Taxi: Metered taxis (e.g., Vinasun, Mai Linh) are available at the official taxi stand outside arrivals. Please use only the official taxi queue.

From hotels in the city center: HCMUT is about 4–5 km away, around 15–25 minutes by taxi or ride-hailing.

Please show the driver the venue name and address in Vietnamese: Trường Đại học Bách khoa – 268 Lý Thường Kiệt.

Accommodation

Participants are responsible for their own accommodation. Athena Hotel is about 1 km from HCMUT. The other three hotels are in central Ho Chi Minh City, about 7 km from the airport and 4–5 km from HCMUT.

Visa

Most international participants need a visa to enter Vietnam. Please check your requirements early.

E-visa: Citizens of all countries and territories can apply online for an e-visa, valid for up to 90 days, single or multiple entry. Apply only through the official portal: evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn. We recommend applying at least 3 weeks before travel.

Visa exemption: Citizens of some countries can enter visa-free for a limited stay. Please check the current list with the Vietnamese embassy or consulate in your country.

Invitation letter: Registered participants who need an invitation letter for their visa application may contact the organizers at conf.cse@hcmut.edu.vn, with their full name, passport number, nationality and paper ID.

Visa rules may change. Please confirm with official sources before you travel.
